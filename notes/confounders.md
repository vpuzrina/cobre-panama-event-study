## Design notes from the checks

- **Day 0 differs by market for intraday or after-hours news.** For news released during TSX hours (E4, E5 pre-open, E6a, F3, F6), the Tokyo, Shenzhen and Hong Kong day 0 is the next local trading day. For F3 and F4, US and European listings have different day-0 dates from TSX (see table).
- **0358.HK and FNV.TO are directly exposed.** Jiangxi Copper owns about 18.5% of FQM, and FNV holds the Cobre Panamá stream. Treat them as affected firms, not as peers.
